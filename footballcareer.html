About Football Career

A single-player football career game focused on building a player from the beginning of their career and progressing through seasons, clubs and competitions. Develop your attributes, earn your place in the team, manage form and fitness, move between clubs and build a career while leagues and clubs continue to change around you.

Current Systems

A selection of systems currently implemented in the playable development build.

  • Player Career — Create your player, choose a position and build a career across multiple seasons, clubs and leagues.
  • Training & Development — Improve Finishing, Passing, Dribbling, Defending and Stamina through XP and training focuses, with development changing as your player ages.
  • Fitness & Injuries — Manage fitness, recovery, fatigue and injuries throughout the season.
  • Manager Selection — Earn starting opportunities, substitute appearances and playing time through form, fitness and performance.
  • Matches & Match Moments — Matches are simulated while position-based moments let you influence goals, assists, defending, match ratings and key situations.
  • Football World — 964 clubs across 30 countries and 60 domestic leagues create a large career world around your player.
  • Promotion & Relegation — Clubs move between domestic league tiers as seasons progress.
  • European Qualification — Domestic league positions determine qualification places for European competition.
  • Transfers & Player Value — Build your market value, receive transfer opportunities and move between clubs during your career.
  • Finances & Lifestyle — Earn money throughout your career and spend it on lifestyle, vehicle and property purchases.
  • Achievements & Statistics — Track career and season statistics, milestones and achievements across multiple seasons.
  • Multiple Career Saves — Keep up to three independent careers with their own player and world progression.
