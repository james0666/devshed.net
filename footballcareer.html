About Football Career

Football Career is a single-player football career game focused on building one player across an evolving football world. Develop your attributes, compete for playing time, manage fitness and injuries, move between clubs and build a career across multiple seasons. Play domestic and European football, earn international selection, compete through World Cup qualifying and experience a career where clubs, leagues and competitions continue to progress around you.

Current Systems

A selection of systems currently implemented in the playable development build.

  • Player Career — Create your player, choose a position and build a career across multiple seasons, clubs, leagues and countries.
  • Training & Development — Improve Finishing, Passing, Dribbling, Defending and Stamina through XP and training focuses, with development changing throughout your career.
  • Fitness & Injuries — Manage fitness, recovery, fatigue and injuries across domestic, European and international football.
  • Selection & Playing Time — Earn starting opportunities, substitute appearances and international call-ups through ability, form, fitness and performance.
  • Matches & Match Moments — Play through position-based Match Moments while the wider match continues around you, with substitutions, commentary, match ratings and key situations integrated into the match flow.
  • Football World — 964 clubs across 30 countries and 60 domestic leagues create a persistent football world that continues to progress around your career.
  • Promotion & Relegation — Clubs move between domestic league tiers as seasons progress.
  • European Competition — Domestic league performance can qualify clubs for Champions League football, including league-phase and knockout progression.
  • International Football — Earn national-team selection and compete through confederation-specific World Cup qualifying systems, international windows and World Cup cycles.
  • Transfers & Player Value — Build your reputation and market value, receive transfer opportunities and move between clubs as your career develops.
  • Finances & Lifestyle — Earn money throughout your career and spend it on vehicle, property and lifestyle purchases.
  • Career History, Achievements & Statistics — Track seasonal performance, career milestones, appearances, goals, assists, trophies and achievements across multiple seasons.
  • Multiple Career Saves — Keep up to three independent careers with their own players, clubs, world states and competition progression.
