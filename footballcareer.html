About Football Career

A single-player football career game focused on building a player from the beginning of their career and progressing through seasons, clubs and competitions. Develop your attributes, earn your place in the team, manage form and fitness, move between clubs and build a career while leagues and clubs continue to change around you.

Current Systems

A selection of systems currently implemented during development.

  • Player Career — Attributes, overall rating, form, fitness, injuries and long-term career statistics.
  • Training & Development — Player progression systems built around improving attributes over a career.
  • League Simulation — Fixtures, results, league tables and long-term club performance.
  • Manager Selection — Starting XI, substitute, squad rotation and form-based selection decisions.
  • Match Moments — Position-based situations that allow the player to influence key moments during matches.
  • Promotion & Relegation — Club movement between domestic league tiers across multiple countries.
  • European Competition — Qualification and progression through continental competition.
  • Transfers — Career movement between clubs through transfer offers and multi-season progression.
  • World Simulation — Background leagues continue to simulate and evolve alongside the player's career.
  • Career Persistence — Save and load support for multi-season player and world progression.
