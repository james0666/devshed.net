About RuralProject

RuralProject is a small-town Australian life sim built around a relaxed pace, simple jobs and the everyday atmosphere of rural-town life.

The project is still in active development, with new buildings, jobs, interactions and world details being added over time.

Current Features

A growing collection of jobs, systems and town interactions already working in the current build.

  • Jobs & Economy — Lawn mowing, deliveries, farming work, cash and bank money, plus optional hidden cash-in-hand jobs.
  • Property & Progression — Earn money around town, buy houses and gradually become more established.
  • Living World — Day and night cycle, weekdays, business opening hours, town lighting and NPC daily routines.
  • Town Interaction — Shops, ATMs, TVs, doors, sleep points and other everyday interactive systems.
  • Player Systems — Energy, inventory, save and load support, banking and property ownership.
  • Rural Atmosphere — Farms, houses, local businesses, animals, lighting and small environmental details.
