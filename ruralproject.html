About RuralProject

RuralProject is a small-town Australian life sim built around a relaxed pace, simple jobs and the everyday atmosphere of rural-town life.

The project is still in active development, with new buildings, jobs, interactions and world details being added over time.

Current Features

A selection of systems and activities currently available in the development build.

  • Rural Setting — Farms, houses, local businesses and animals.
  • Jobs & Economy — Lawn mowing, deliveries, farming work, banking and cash jobs.
  • Property Ownership — Buy houses and unlock access to owned property.
  • Living World — Day and night cycle, weekdays, business hours and NPC routines.
  • Player Systems — Energy, simple inventory, sleep and save/load support.
  • Interactive World — Doors, ATMs, TVs and other basic interactions.
